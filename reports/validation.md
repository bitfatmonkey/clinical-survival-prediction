## Scientific cautions

The training Cox global PH test has p=0.0201. There are significant term-level departures, and the benchmark is retained with explicit caution rather than revised after observing test performance. Only 35 test patients remain at risk at five years; calibration-group risk sets are smaller still. These results are educational, conditional on fitted models and not externally validated for contemporary UK/NHS care.
